FAQ

Good questions.
Actual answers.

The practical side, all in one place. Read this before reaching out. I like a little mystery in my reading; booking me should involve considerably less guesswork.

Before you reach out: send a completed booking form or an email containing every detail requested on it. Incomplete requests will not receive a response. Once I send my booking response, your deposit is due within 24 hours. Your date is not held until it is received.

Booking & Inquiries

How to begin.

How do I book time with you?
Complete the booking form, provide the requested screening information, and follow through with the booking steps. Prefer email? Your message must include every detail requested on the form. I will not respond to incomplete booking requests.
What should I include in my inquiry?
Include every detail requested on the booking form, including your name, requested date, city or location, length of time, overall plan, and all requested screening information. Please use the form as your checklist when emailing. A charming introduction is lovely. It does not replace the details.
Do you prefer the booking form or direct email?
The form is preferred. Email is welcome only when it includes all the same information. No completed form or complete email means no response. I will not chase down missing answers.
Do you offer a more discreet contact option?
Yes. For more discreet parties, I can also be reached at keikiconnoisseur@protonmail.ch. If you choose that route, please still include everything requested on my booking form. Replies there may take a bit longer.
How long does it take to hear back from you?
Response time depends on my schedule and message volume. Please allow time for a reply to a complete, respectful inquiry. Incomplete requests will not receive a response; sending them again does not change that.
Do you respond to all inquiries?
No. I will not respond to incomplete booking requests, requests for a menu of intimate services, explicit questions, or proposals involving unlawful activity. My compensation is for my time only. Please keep inquiries focused on booking details.
What if I start arranging a booking and don’t follow through?
Please begin the booking process only when you intend to follow through. If you abandon the process or stop responding while we are arranging your booking, I will not accept future bookings from you. My time matters before the date, too. If plans change, communicate promptly and follow the cancellation policy below.
Do you accept last-minute requests?
Sometimes. Advance notice gives us more options and makes planning easier.

Deposits & Payment

Booking policy.

Is a deposit required?
Yes. A deposit is required to confirm your booking. Your date is not held until the deposit is received.
How soon do I need to send my deposit?
Your deposit must be received within 24 hours of my booking response. Please be ready to send it when you inquire. Until it arrives, your booking is unconfirmed and your requested date remains available for someone else to book. If the deposit is not received within that window, you will not be booked and the date will not be held. An email exchange is lovely; it is not a reservation.
How much is the deposit?
For bookings under 3 hours, the deposit is $100. For bookings of 3 hours or more, the deposit is 50%. Travel bookings require 50% in advance.
What payment methods do you accept?
Deposits may be sent via Cash App, Venmo, Zelle, or BTC.
Are deposits refundable?
Deposits are non-refundable unless I cancel, in which case the deposit is returned in full.
What happens if I need to cancel?
If a cancellation occurs within 72 hours, 50% of the deposit is owed. If a cancellation occurs within 24 hours, the full deposit is owed.
When is the remaining balance due?
Final details are handled privately once a booking is confirmed.

Do you need downtime during longer travel bookings?
Yes. Longer itineraries need room for rest, meals, movement, and my personal routine. Please build that into the plan.
