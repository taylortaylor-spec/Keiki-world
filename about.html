Presence

Curiosity is a very attractive habit.

Books that give me something to think about. Cooking something worth sitting down for. A good workout. Getting dressed up for dinner. Going somewhere new and finding the little place we’ll still be talking about afterward.

I’m highly creative, naturally curious, and usually working on something. Ask what I’m making and you may get an enthusiastic explanation. Ask why it isn’t finished and suddenly I believe in privacy. Tell me what you’re reading, building, or obsessed with lately.

Sensitive enough to write poetry. Sarcastic enough to deny it was about you.

Details

My kind of date.

Make a reservation somewhere you’re excited to take me. Let’s try something new, talk about more than our schedules, and see whether dessert turns into another hour of conversation. I’m very supportive of dessert having ambitions.

Dates
Leave room
to get distracted.
A bookstore before dinner. An exhibition we can discuss afterward. A show, a shopping outing, or a weekend away with no need to rush. A beautiful hotel and a slow morning are persuasive too. Some of my multitudes would like room service.
Win me over
Good manners.
A little initiative.
Be kind, generous, and reliable. “I’ve made the reservation” is a remarkably good sentence. Remember what I like, ask what makes me comfortable, and treat my time with care. I notice how you treat the people who aren’t your date.
The softer side
Yes, the sarcasm
comes with feelings.
You can tease me. I’ll almost certainly tease you back. There’s a sensitive person underneath it: I notice small things, care deeply, and remember more than is convenient for either of us. Warmth matters to me.
